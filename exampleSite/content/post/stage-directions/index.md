This post is the front door of the example site. Every page you can reach from here is drawn by the Stage theme, with only the settings in `exampleSite/hugo.toml`.

## The frame and the paper

The **frame** is loud: a red stage with a halftone, black slabs, and letters cut from magazines. The **paper**, where you read, is quiet: one serif face, a comfortable measure, high contrast, and it follows your system's light or dark setting.

Titles are tape strips. The part before the colon goes on a white strip, the rest on a red one, so a title like this one splits in two.

## Motion, and how to stop it

Panels slam in, notes drop, the starburst turns. All of it:

- stops when your system asks for reduced motion;
- can be paused with the *Pause motion* button on the home page;
- can be turned off for the whole site with `params.stage.motion.enabled = false`.

The reading-progress bar at the top is the one exception: it moves only when you scroll.
